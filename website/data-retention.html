Overview

This policy describes how long AgentAudit retains customer data and how you can request deletion. It applies to all data submitted through the website, API, SDKs, or dashboard.

Retention periods

Active account data

While your account is active, we retain your organization profile, API key records, compliance rules, audit logs, alerts, and billing records. You can update most of this data from the dashboard at any time.

Audit logs

By default, audit logs remain until account deletion or an approved deletion request. Every plan supports a retention preference of 7, 30, or 90 days, or until account deletion. Existing accounts keep their current preference unless they change it. Automatic age-based cleanup is currently disabled: saving a preference does not start deletion. We will notify users before enabling automatic cleanup.

When cleanup is enabled, a selected period applies to existing and future logs, based on their creation time in UTC. Associated alerts expire with their log; standalone alerts expire by their own creation date. Cleanup runs in bounded batches, so eligibility is not a promise of deletion at an exact instant. Shortening a period requires an impact preview and password confirmation in the dashboard. Increasing it cannot restore deleted data. Agents, policies, rules, billing records, and monthly usage are not affected.

Reports and delivered copies

Saved reports store report settings, not frozen copies of audit data. Each download is generated from the logs still available in the requested date range. Later downloads can differ after deletion or retention cleanup; older trace links may also stop resolving. Download records you need before they expire. Previously delivered emails, webhooks, and downloaded files are not recalled by cleanup.

Email delivery records

Email delivery records support deduplication, delivery-status tracking, and support inquiries. There is currently no automatic 90-day cleanup job for these records. They remain until account deletion or an approved deletion request, subject to the exceptions below. A new delivery record may be created for the account-deletion confirmation email. Provider-held email records and delivered messages are separate from our active database.

API keys

API keys are displayed once in plain text and then stored only as keyed hashes. Revoked keys are retained in hashed form to preserve audit history and prevent reuse. Raw keys are never recoverable.

Backups

Deletion from the active database does not immediately remove backup copies. Our daily logical-backup workflow targets removal of backups older than 30 days, and an S3 lifecycle rule (`expire-backups-30d`, scoped to the `postgres/` prefix) independently expires backup objects 30 days after creation. Railway native automatic backup schedules are currently disabled. A retained manual snapshot is older than 30 days and is not covered by the lifecycle rule or pruning job. Manual snapshots require separate review and approved deletion. Contact support for backup-retention details.

How to request deletion

Self-serve deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the dashboard (look for Delete Organization in your organization settings) or by calling the API directly:

DELETE /api/v1/auth/organization
Authorization: Bearer <your-jwt>
Content-Type: application/json

{ "password": "your-account-password" }

This removes your organization, API keys, agents, audit logs, policies, compliance rules, saved report settings, alerts, and existing organization-linked email delivery records from the active database. A new delivery record may be created for the deletion-confirmation email. Stripe subscription cancellation and customer deletion are attempted when billing is configured; failures require operator follow-up. Stripe-held billing records, backups, and previously delivered or downloaded copies are separate.

Email request

You can also request deletion by emailing us at support@agentaudit.online from the email address associated with your account. We will confirm the request, delete the data within 30 days, and notify you when deletion is complete.

Exceptions

We may retain data beyond the standard periods if:

  • We are required to do so by law or legal process.
  • The data is necessary for an active investigation of abuse, security incident, or dispute.
  • The data is aggregated or anonymized so that it can no longer identify you or your organization.

Changes

We may update this policy as retention practices change. We will notify you of material changes by email or through a prominent notice on the website.

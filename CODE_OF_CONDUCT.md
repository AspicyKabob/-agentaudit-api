# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AgentAudit aims to be a welcoming, respectful community. Everyone participating in this project is expected to treat others with dignity, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or level of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different perspectives.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not personal attacks.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help resolve misunderstandings.
- Respect privacy. Use synthetic examples and never share another person's private information or customer data without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexualized behavior, personal insults, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed spaces, and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to [support@agentaudit.online](mailto:support@agentaudit.online) with the subject `Code of conduct report`. Include relevant context and links if safe to do so. Do not post sensitive details in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect the reporter's privacy as far as reasonably possible. Information will be shared only as needed to investigate and respond. Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or edit inappropriate contributions and may issue a warning, limit participation, or temporarily or permanently exclude participants based on the severity and pattern of behavior. They will consider context, explain decisions to affected participants where appropriate, and apply these expectations to maintainers as well as contributors.

Anyone affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration through the same private reporting address, providing relevant additional context.
